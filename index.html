Burge — Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 6, 2026 · Developer: Cihan Mert Bayam

Summary:
  • No account, no tracking, no ads, no sale of data.
  • The name you enter at onboarding never leaves your device.
  • Searches are logged under a random anonymous tag (e.g. swift-fox-42) that cannot be linked to you.
  • Your precise GPS coordinates are used only to run your search — they are not stored on our servers.

1. What Burge does

Burge is a mobile app that shows you a short list of the best-rated places (cafés, restaurants, bars…) around a location you choose. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

2. Data we collect

Search logs. When you run a search, we record:

  • the search text you typed (e.g. “cafe”),
  • the location text shown in the app (a city, district, or street-level area name — not your exact coordinates),
  • the search mode and how many results were found,
  • the Google place IDs of the top results (identifiers only — no place names or ratings are stored),
  • a random anonymous session tag (e.g. swift-fox-42).

The anonymous tag is generated randomly on your device, is not derived from your name or any device identifier, and is shown to you inside the app (“Your ID” on the home screen). We cannot connect it to your identity.

Location. If you tap “Use my location”, the app reads your device’s GPS coordinates with your permission and uses them as the center of your search. Coordinates are sent to Google to perform the search and to resolve an approximate area name for display. Your precise coordinates are not stored on our servers; only the approximate area name may appear in the search log. Location access is optional — you can always type a location instead — and you can revoke the permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.

Data that stays on your device only (never sent to our servers): the display name you enter at onboarding, your daily search counter, and your recent searches.

We do not collect: your name, email address, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, or any analytics/tracking data. We do not sell or share data for advertising.

3. Where data is stored and why

Search logs are stored in Supabase, a hosted database service. We use them solely to understand aggregate usage and improve result quality. They are not linked to your identity, not used for advertising, and not shared with third parties beyond the service providers named in this policy.

4. Google Maps Platform

Burge uses Google Maps Platform services (Places API and Geocoding API) to find places and resolve locations. This application includes Google Maps features and content. Use of Google Maps features and content is subject to the then-current versions of:

  • Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service
  • Google Privacy Policy

When the app makes requests to Google, Google collects and receives data such as search terms, IP addresses, and latitude/longitude coordinates, as described in the Google Privacy Policy. We do not send Google your anonymous tag or any name you entered in the app.

5. Data retention and deletion

Search logs are kept only as long as needed for the purposes above. You can request deletion of the logs associated with your anonymous tag (visible as “Your ID” at the bottom of the app’s home screen) by emailing us at the address below; include the tag in your message.

6. Children

Burge is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
